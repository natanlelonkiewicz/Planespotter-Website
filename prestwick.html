ABOUT THE AIRPORT

Plane Spotting at Glasgow Prestwick

Glasgow Prestwick Airport is located in South Ayrshire, Scotland, and is a popular destination for aviation photographers and plane spotters.

The airport can see a mixture of passenger traffic, cargo aircraft, military visitors and occasional unusual aircraft movements, making it an interesting location for aviation enthusiasts.

The best spotting location depends on the active runway, wind direction, time of day and position of the sun. AVSPOT helps you plan your visit by bringing this information together in one place.

WHERE TO SPOT

Best Spotting Locations

AVSPOT currently includes several spotting locations around Glasgow Prestwick Airport, each offering a different view of aircraft movements and lighting conditions.

MOUND

An elevated spotting location near the tower, offering clear views of aircraft operating on Runway 30. A good choice for photographing arrivals, departures and general airfield activity.

BRIDGE

A location offering a different angle on aircraft movements, with good opportunities to photograph aircraft approaching or lining up on runway 12 depending on the lighting conditions.

MONKTON

Located on the opposite side of the airport, Monkton can provide better angles for aircraft using Runway 12 in the morning and offers a useful alternative when lighting is less favourable at the other locations.
