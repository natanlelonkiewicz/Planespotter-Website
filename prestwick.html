Glasgow Prestwick Airport Information

Airport: Glasgow Prestwick Airport

ICAO: EGPK

IATA: PIK

Glasgow Prestwick Airport is located in South Ayrshire, Scotland, and is known for its regular passenger traffic, cargo aircraft, military visitors and occasional unusual aircraft movements.

Plane Spotting at Glasgow Prestwick Airport

Prestwick is a popular location for aviation enthusiasts and photographers. Different spotting locations can provide views of aircraft arriving, departing and taxiing around the airport.

The best location can depend on the active runway, wind direction, time of day and position of the sun.

Aviation Photography Conditions

Lighting is one of the most important factors when photographing aircraft. The position of the sun changes throughout the day, meaning a spotting location that is perfectly lit in the morning may be backlit later in the day.

AVSPOT provides live photography and lighting information to help you choose a suitable spotting location.
