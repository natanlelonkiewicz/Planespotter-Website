About Avspot

Avspot is a website created to help aircraft enthusiasts and planespotters find and make the most of aircraft spotting locations.

I created Avspot because finding good spotting locations can often involve a lot of searching, local knowledge and trial and error. The aim of Avspot is to bring useful information together in one place, making it easier for spotters to discover locations and plan their visits.

What Avspot provides

  • Aircraft spotting locations
  • Runway information
  • Recommended focal lengths
  • Parking and access information
  • Best times to visit
  • Current weather conditions
  • Photography and lighting conditions
  • Aircraft and flight information where available

Built for the spotting community

A major part of Avspot is the knowledge shared by aircraft spotters themselves. Local information can make a huge difference when visiting an unfamiliar airport, particularly when it comes to access, parking, fences, viewpoints and changing conditions.

Avspot is intended to grow alongside the spotting community. Information and photographs can be contributed by spotters to help improve the information available to other enthusiasts.

Who runs Avspot?

Avspot was created and is maintained by Natan Lelonkiewicz, an aircraft enthusiast and photographer.

The website is an independent project created to make aircraft spotting information easier to find and more useful for enthusiasts.

Our goal

The goal of Avspot is simple: make plane spotting easier.

As the website develops, I plan to continue adding airports, spotting locations, useful tools and community features.

If you have information that could help improve an airport or spotting location listed on Avspot, you can get in touch through the Contact Us page.

Thank you for visiting Avspot.
