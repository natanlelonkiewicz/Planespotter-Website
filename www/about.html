About Avspot

Avspot is a platform created for aircraft enthusiasts and planespotters to discover spotting locations, explore aviation information and make the most of their time spotting aircraft.

I created Avspot because finding good spotting locations can often involve a lot of searching, local knowledge and trial and error. The aim of Avspot is to bring useful information together in one place, making it easier for spotters to discover locations, plan visits and keep track of their aviation experiences.

What Avspot provides

  • Aircraft spotting locations and detailed location information
  • Airport and runway information
  • Recommended focal lengths and photography information
  • Parking, access and viewpoint information
  • Recommended times to visit
  • Current weather conditions
  • Photography and lighting conditions
  • Aircraft and flight information where available
  • Low Flying Areas and low-level spotting information
  • Plane Logs for recording aircraft you have seen
  • Community submissions and suggested edits
  • Optional notifications for supported spotting features

Built for the spotting community

A major part of Avspot is the knowledge shared by aircraft spotters themselves. Local information can make a huge difference when visiting an unfamiliar airport or spotting location, particularly when it comes to access, parking, fences, viewpoints and changing conditions.

Avspot allows members of the community to contribute information, photographs and suggestions that can help improve the platform for everyone.

Community submissions can include airports, spotting locations, Low Flying Areas, descriptions, aircraft information and photographs. Suggested edits can also be submitted to help keep existing information accurate and up to date.

Avspot accounts

Avspot offers optional accounts that provide access to additional features and allow users to contribute to the platform.

Registered users can manage their account, create Plane Logs, submit new information, suggest edits and use other account-based features as Avspot develops.

Plane Logs

Plane Logs allow spotters to keep track of the aircraft they have seen and record their own spotting experiences.

The feature is designed to give spotters a simple way to build and maintain their own personal aviation records while using Avspot.

Avspot Plus

Avspot Plus is an optional upgrade that provides access to additional features on the platform.

Avspot Plus is available as a one-time purchase and is designed to support the continued development and maintenance of Avspot while giving users access to additional features.

Who runs Avspot?

Avspot was created and is maintained by Natan Lelonkiewicz, an aircraft enthusiast and photographer.

Avspot is an independent project created to make aircraft spotting information easier to find, more useful and more accessible to enthusiasts.

Our goal

The goal of Avspot is simple: make plane spotting easier.

I want Avspot to continue growing into a useful platform for aircraft enthusiasts, combining spotting locations, aviation information, personal logging and community knowledge in one place.

As Avspot develops, I plan to continue adding more locations, useful tools and features while improving the information already available.

Get involved

If you have information that could improve a location on Avspot, you can contribute through the available submission and edit features.

Whether you are visiting a new airport, exploring a Low Flying Area or simply keeping track of the aircraft you have seen, Avspot is built to help make the experience easier.

Thank you for visiting Avspot and being part of the spotting community.
